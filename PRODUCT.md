# Gusto

## Register
product — design serves the product. This is a CRM app, not a marketing surface.

## Product Purpose
Gusto is a calmer CRM for the people and companies you care about. Self-hosted-friendly, dependency-light, designed to feel like a quiet desk reference rather than a sales tool. The user pastes links (LinkedIn, X, GitHub, company sites, etc.); Gusto saves and enriches the entity in the background and shows it in a dense, keyboard-driven list.

## Users
Individual operators (founders, recruiters, investors, generalists) who maintain a personal network of people and companies. They live in the keyboard, value speed over chrome, and don't want a CRM that nags or upsells. They expect inline editing, fast filters, and tables that read clearly at a glance.

## Brand & tone
Warm, paper-grade, ink-on-cream. Confident but quiet. The accent color is graphite-black, never a hue. Type is Geist (variable) with tabular numerics for tables. Copy is terse and human, never marketing-flavored. No emojis. No exclamation points.

## Anti-references
- Salesforce / HubSpot — busy, colorful, modal-heavy, dashboardy.
- Notion-as-CRM templates — too many empty database fields, too much chrome.
- Apple Contacts — too sparse, no enrichment, no list density.
- Crypto / SaaS-default neon-on-black.

## Strategic principles
- **Lightweight by default.** Every dependency is a footprint decision; the app must run on a 1 GB VPS.
- **Keyboard first.** j/k/Enter/*/# navigate, open, favorite, archive. The mouse is a fallback.
- **Inline edit beats route changes.** Status, priority, tags, sector, size — all editable from the row.
- **Empty data is normal.** Most rows will be partly empty; the design must look intentional, not broken, when cells are blank.
- **Calm hierarchy.** Identity (name) dominates; metadata is quiet; interactive pills sit at the right.
